# Andrej Karpathy

Summary: AI researcher and educator known for work in deep learning, computer vision, and autonomous agents. Author of the LLM Wiki conceptual pattern.

## Background and Relevance

- Founding member of OpenAI and former Director of AI at Tesla.
- Proponent of using LLM agents as persistent knowledge base compilers rather than isolated query-time retrieval tools.
- Formulated the three-layer LLM Wiki architecture (Raw sources, Wiki, Schema) paired with Obsidian for visualization.
