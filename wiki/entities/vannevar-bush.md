# Vannevar Bush

Summary: American engineer and administrator who published the seminal 1945 essay "As We May Think", introducing the conceptual Memex device that influenced hypertext and personal knowledge management systems.

## Historical Connection to LLM Wiki

- Envisioned an individual record machine creating associative trails between documents, mirroring modern hypertext and wikilinks.
- The fundamental bottleneck in the Memex vision was the manual maintenance burden of building and sustaining associative trails.
- The LLM Wiki pattern addresses this historical limitation by delegating cross-referencing, linking, and synthesis maintenance to autonomous LLM agents.
